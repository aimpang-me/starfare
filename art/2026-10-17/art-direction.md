# Art direction — Quiet passage

**Make a place passengers would want to travel.** Broad enclosed volumes, visible lounges, restrained window ribbons and readable structural gaps should carry the identity. Keep surfaces calm enough to coexist with orbital maps and dense operational text. These are proposals, not approved product replacements.

## Shared visual language

| Element | Specification |
|---|---|
| Space / negative space | Navy-black #050a14; sparse stars; orbital context subordinate to the asset |
| Main hull | Matte navy #0b1423 with broad quiet panels and graphite recesses |
| Structure | Satin alloy near #a2b3c8; selective bright edges establish silhouette |
| Flight accents | Cool cyan #56cefa in narrow seams and small fixture details |
| Human / solar warmth | Warm #ffc06a in cabin windows and restrained illustrative solar rim light |
| Display text | #e9f0f8; production provenance remains readable HTML/text owned by Design |
| Shape grammar | Blunt bows, enclosed passenger volumes, deliberate negative space; no weapons or aggressive spikes |
| Lighting | Neutral readable key; warm edge; limited bloom; dark engine interiors by default |

Painted lighting is illustrative. Production lighting derived from real sky geometry must come from Engineering's data, not these plates. The art does not establish how the ships fly, generate gravity, support life or perform.

## Individual concepts

**Kestrel — compact delta.** Keep the broad arrowhead body, uninterrupted glazed lounge and split rear notch. Its silhouette should remain legible when panel seams disappear. Current risks: aircraft-like shape and dense panel treatment. Next refinement should soften the rear structures and reduce fine seams. No seat count or shuttle capability is assigned.

**Tandem — open-center liner.** Preserve two parallel passenger hulls, one broad forward bridge, a lighter rear bridge and a generous central opening. This is the recommended visual signature: recognizable through negative space and clearly civilian through the lounges. Reconcile bridge heights and front-end shapes before modeling. No capacity or service-range advantage is asserted.

**Lantern — inhabited spine.** Preserve observation cabin, three separated passenger modules and triangular tail frame. The center should read as passenger accommodation, not cargo or armament. Current paintings do not establish exact radial geometry; redraw a consistent top/side/front set before modeling. No long-range or propulsion claim is assigned.

**Wayfarer — open concourse.** Preserve the C silhouette, blunt open ends, segmented warm concourse and offset service spine. Do not close the central gap or add a rotating ring without a separately approved concept and data contract. Collars in the art do not establish available berths or actual docking compatibility.

## Asset delivery specification for a later selected direction

These are proposed deliverables, not files already produced. Sizes are image-delivery targets, not spacecraft dimensions.

| Deliverable | Proposed export | Art requirements |
|---|---|---|
| Hero / fleet detail | 1536 × 1024 opaque plate, plus transparent subject export | Full silhouette with safe margins; omit embedded title from reusable asset |
| Small fleet portrait | 512 × 512 transparent PNG, approved WebP derivative | Simplified panels; no baked status light; inspect at 96px and 48px |
| Map symbol | Original simplified vector after selection | Distinct topology at 24px/32px; Design owns integration and legibility |
| Model reference | Consistent front/top/side plus three-quarter view | One resolved geometry; no claimed measurements; images here are not that sheet |
| Effect masks | Separate transparent navigation, berth and propulsion layers | Unlit base; no effects baked into hull; explicit named attachment points |
| Reduced-motion view | Static state frames and text labels | Identical state meaning without pulsing, travel tween or particles |

Proposed effect attachment names: `nav_port`, `nav_starboard`, `nav_aft`, `dock_collar`, `main_nozzle`. Names are art-side proposals only; Engineering defines mappings and orientation conventions. Do not infer actual orientation or thrust direction from screen-space silhouette.

## Provenance and layout boundaries

- **Fictional:** these ships, station, names and illustrated backgrounds. Keep an explicit label adjacent to any public concept preview.
- **Simulated:** engine-sourced state and events when provided. A fictional vehicle can have a simulated journey; retain both labels rather than collapsing them.
- **Real:** only separately sourced and verified astronomical/context data. Generated planet imagery is never a real-data layer.
- Labels must be words, not color alone. Keep simulation UI accents distinct from warm cabin materials through placement and text.
- Assets must fit Orbital Desk, Departure Lounge or Mission Control without a layout change. Small thumbnails must not push Bookings or compact activity down the phone screen. No autoplay hero is required.
- Local concept previews never create agent/simulation events, flight counts or booking state.
- No Tesla, SpaceX, Grok or xAI logos; no implied affiliation. Names are working concept names, without trademark-clearance claims.
