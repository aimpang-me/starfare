Trips: the day-4 agent run (a dispatcher reads the request, an engineer calls the trip planner, a broker writes the answer), replayed here. Each trip is a planner output that an independent n-body checker (Sun, eight planets and the Moon) re-flew before it was offered. Planet positions: JPL DE421. The Roadster: a JPL Horizons state, carried forward around the Sun.

    
Drawn, not computed: Titan's place in its orbit, the car's place around Titan, Saturn's rings, and the short hop from Titan onto the Sun-centred route. Light delays on this page are computed from those positions.

    

    
For scale: the fastest capsule ever to enter Earth's atmosphere, NASA's Stardust in 2006, came in at about 12.9 km/s. Every ride here arrives faster than that.
