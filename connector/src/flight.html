      Starfare is a passenger-flight game and agent platform by Alvin Impang. Its passengers, fleet and hazards are simulated, and it isn't affiliated with any real company. Flight numbers come from the run log or engine. Transfer planning uses an impulsive approximation; propulsion feasibility is not yet validated. Paths are interpolated for playback; older records may need reconstructed paths. Models and assumptions.

      Planet positions: NASA JPL DE421. Sky time runs fast: one real second is one sky day, and the sky resets at the end of each season.
