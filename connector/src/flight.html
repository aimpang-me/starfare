    Starfare is a concept demo by Alvin Impang. It's a made-up robotaxi service with simulated passengers, a simulated fleet and simulated hazards, and it isn't affiliated with any real company. This flight was flown in the simulation through the Starfare connector; every number on this page comes from its run log or from the engine.

    Planet positions: NASA JPL DE421. Sky time runs fast: one real second is one sky day, and the sky resets at the end of each season.
