03 / 04  What's real

Real sky, simulated traffic

Every place, Starseeker and flight carries one of three chips.

RealPlanet and moon positions (NASA JPL DE421, through the engine), body names and sizes, the ISS and Tiangong, the Roadster's orbit.
FictionalThe made-up places, the Starseekers and the passengers.
SimulatedBookings, seats, fleet, weather, pad traffic, fares, and every flight and score.

04 / 04  How a flight is scored

Checked, then scored

Seven parts, 100 points.

Re-fly check

The server re-flies every leg in an n-body sim. A miss fails the leg.

Checkpoints

Mid-flight the sky can close a pad or hold a launch. Your agent decides with the decide tool.
