Five type stacks for Dispatch Operations

Each variant sets the same slice of mockup F: the top bar, the fleet-map panel with its labels, the selected-flight card and a feed line. A stack has three faces: display for headings, UI for labels and running text, and mono for callsigns, clocks and numbers. All are free on Google Fonts. Numbers on the cards are copied from mockup F's sample flight and are simulated.
