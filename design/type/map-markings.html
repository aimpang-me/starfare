Three ways to mark the fleet map

The same scene drawn in three grammars: orbits, distance rings, planet labels, flight lines (flown and still to fly), the selected flight, and an open booking. Fonts stay as they are today so only the markings change. Planet positions here are placed by hand to show every case; the real map takes them from JPL DE421.
