## Concrete changes

1. Remove repeated card borders and technical decoration. Three content columns are separated by space and two hairlines.
2. Make route names and states legible at first glance. Use full labels, restrained metadata and tabular numerals only where useful.
3. Keep white as the primary text/action hierarchy; cyan marks selection and route information; amber marks simulation conditions. No brand logos or implied affiliation.
4. Use NASA Earth/Moon imagery with attribution, and a built-in-imagegen cutout derived from Art's Kestrel design. Preserve Art originals and record the derivative. No fictional astronomical measurements.
5. Mobile starts with bookings and compact activity. Map expands on demand. Metadata remains readable and controls are at least 44px high.
6. Provide inspectable sample, empty, loading and unavailable states. A stale snapshot never looks live.

## Motion thesis

The authored moment is selecting a booking: its details and route highlight update together in one 180ms opacity transition. It communicates selection, not elapsed flight time. Dialog entry is 220ms with a 6px translation; exit is 140ms. Focus and feedback are immediate. No idle float, pulse, parallax, orbit spin, thruster glow or automatic map travel.

Replay uses explicit, timestamped sample records, advances by record on user input and labels gaps. There is no spatial interpolation, autoplay or eased simulation clock. Reduced motion removes translations and fades; controls and status changes remain clear. Hidden documents cancel transient UI animations. Integration will bind only to Engineering's recorded positions, states and events after PR #13; no frontend-derived departure/burn/arrival inference is permitted.

## Boundary

All work is confined to this standalone artifact directory. Application source, Engineering's files and Art's originals remain untouched. Asset/design fidelity is the deliverable; production data integration is not. Final ship-class selection remains Art/product review, not implied approval of a fleet model.
