Four Claude models ran the same 20 rider requests with the same planner and the same gates. No made-up number got through: the one struck answer was a bare year Sonnet wrote. They differ in how much they help. Opus and Fable offered 15 verified rides each, Sonnet 13 and Haiku 6, because Haiku asked or declined on 14 of 20. The checker threw out the same bad Roadster route for Sonnet and for Fable, and both then found one that passed.
